My First Custom ROM: Samsung Galaxy J7 (2015)

In late 2025, I got into custom ROMs and decided to revive my old 2015 Samsung Galaxy J7 (which was super laggy whenever connected to Wi-Fi).

First, I removed all my Google Accounts to prevent FRP (Factory Reset Protection) lock and backed up all my photos to an external 1TB hard drive. When I tried flashing TWRP, the PC didn't detect the phone—turned out I forgot to install the Samsung USB drivers! Once the drivers were installed, I flashed TWRP, wiped the phone, and tried installing LineageOS 18.

It kept throwing errors over and over until the phone soft-bricked. Without following a tutorial, the phone got stuck prompting to connect to Samsung Smart Switch. After realizing the desktop version was discontinued, I researched and found Frija, entered my phone's IMEI/Serial Number, downloaded the stock firmware, and unbricked it with Odin.

Lesson learned! I followed a proper tutorial the second time around, flashed Lineage Recovery (which solved the TWRP error), and successfully installed LineageOS 18 alongside GApps. Flashing my first custom ROM and seeing it boot up felt amazing—I was super proud of making it work!
